# COIN-OP ALLIANCE: DAO GOVERNANCE FRAMEWORK

**Entity:** Skill Wager DAO  
**Token:** $PLAY  
**Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Introduction

To prevent central point-of-failure liability regarding game compliance and censorship, control over the Skill Wager Title Registry and global economic parameters is transitioned to the Coin-Op Alliance DAO.

## 2. Voting Power & Delegation

- **Voting power** is directly proportional to staked $PLAY tokens.
- **Node Operators:** Venues that lock $PLAY in their Node Treasury receive a **1.2x voting multiplier**, ensuring the physical infrastructure providers maintain strong network influence.
- **Delegation:** Players can delegate their voting power to trusted figures (e.g., prominent eSports players or major Publisher Guilds) to vote on their behalf.

## 3. Proposal Categories

### Type 1: Game Compliance & Audits (The Registry)

Before a new Tier A/B game is allowed on the network, it must pass a DAO vote.

**Process:** A publisher submits a game to the portal. Independent DAO auditors review the code/manifest to ensure it meets the "Predominant Skill Mandate" and does not violate IP laws.

**Execution:** A successful vote triggers an on-chain transaction that adds the game's SHA-256 hash to the OmniEscrow smart contract whitelist.

### Type 2: Economic Parameter Adjustments

The DAO controls the economic levers of the platform to maintain market competitiveness.

- **Adjusting the Rake:** Proposing changes to the 7% gross rake or the 2/2/3 distribution split.
- **Adjusting the Synthetic AMM:** Tuning the difficulty multipliers (e.g., shifting the Hard mode multiplier from 1.5x to 1.6x based on global win-rate telemetry).

### Type 3: Ecosystem Grants

The DAO manages the 40% Community Rewards treasury. Proposals can be submitted to fund:
- eSports prize pools
- Host global arcade tournaments
- Provide development grants to indie studios building native Web3 SDK games
