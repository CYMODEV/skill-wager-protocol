# $PLAY TOKENOMICS & INITIAL DISTRIBUTION

**Ticker:** PLAY  
**Standard:** ERC-20 (Deployed natively on L2)  
**Total Supply:** 1,000,000,000 (1 Billion)  
**Version:** 1.0.0

## 1. Utility of $PLAY

$PLAY is the lifeblood of the Skill Wager ecosystem. It functions strictly as a utility and settlement token for the network:

1. **Escrow Liquidity:** Used by players to fund peer-to-peer competitive wagers.
2. **Network Rake Distribution:** Used to autonomously compensate Venue Nodes and Game Developers.
3. **Spectator Staking:** Used to provide parimutuel liquidity to broadcasted tournaments.
4. **Governance (Phase 3):** Used by the Coin-Op Alliance DAO to vote on protocol updates and Tier A game approvals.

## 2. Token Allocation & Vesting

The genesis supply of 1 Billion $PLAY is allocated to ensure long-term ecosystem health and prevent early dumping.

| Category | Allocation | Amount | Vesting Schedule |
|----------|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Community & Ecosystem Rewards | 40% | 400,000,000 | Unlocked linearly over 60 months. Used for CPA Bounties, LP incentives, and tournament prizes. |
| Core Team & Founders | 20% | 200,000,000 | 12-month cliff, then linear vesting over 36 months. |
| Investors (Seed & Series A) | 15% | 150,000,000 | 6-month cliff, then linear vesting over 24 months. |
| Treasury & Operational Liquidity | 15% | 150,000,000 | 20% unlocked at TGE for AMM pool funding. Remainder unlocks linearly over 48 months. |
| Strategic Partners & Publishers | 10% | 100,000,000 | Vested based on performance milestones (e.g., Active Daily Players metric). |

## 3. The Deflationary "Treasury Burn" Mechanism

To counter inflationary pressures from Community Rewards, the protocol implements a deflationary sink:

- The global protocol rake is **7%**. Of that, **3%** goes to the Skill Wager Treasury.
- Every quarter, the DAO executes a smart contract that takes **20% of the accrued Treasury Rake** and permanently burns it.
- This creates a direct correlation between platform volume and token scarcity.
